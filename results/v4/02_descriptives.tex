\subsection{1.1 Description of the Data Gathering
Process}\label{description-of-the-data-gathering-process}

The data considered in the project is from OpenAlex. We use a pair-based
keyword search to identify articles which use or construct
game-theoretical models. The keywords we are taken from two lists: 1. A
list of JEL codes defining the subject of study, i.e.~game theory. 2. A
list of terms describing game setup which ensures that we only cover
papers employing or constructing game-theortic models. For each pair of
keywords, we identify articles based on the appearance of keyword pairs
in the title or abstract of the paper (\color{red} maybe we also want to
consider full texts? This would increase the number of articles
drastically.\color{black}) Currently, we focus only on articles and
reviews written in English (\color{red}Should we include
books?\color{black}). We drop articles from the corpus without any
reference information. As the number of articles is rather low prior to
1980, we choose this year as the starting point for our analysis. In
this version (``v4''), we furthermore consider only articles which have
journal information and whose journals are listed by the USNSF in order
to assign disciplines to journals similar to Truc et al.~(2023).
